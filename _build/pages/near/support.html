Frequently asked questions

The guide is not speaking when I reach a place.

NEAR needs location access to know where you are. Open Settings → NEAR → Location and allow access. Also check that a walk is running.

It does not hear my questions.

Allow microphone and speech recognition access in Settings → NEAR. If you muted the guide with the microphone button on the main screen or lock screen, tap it again to let it listen.

How do I stop it listening?

Tap the microphone button on the main screen or on the lock-screen card. The guide remembers this choice between walks. The microphone is never open outside a walk.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything NEAR keeps on your phone. We do not hold personal data on our servers; see the Privacy Policy for details.
