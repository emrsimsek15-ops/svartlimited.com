NEAR

An audio walking guide for iPhone.

Start a walk, put your phone in your pocket, and NEAR tells you the story of the places around you as you reach them. When something catches your interest, just ask — out loud, without taking the phone out.

What it does

  • Stories as you arrive. NEAR notices when you reach somewhere worth hearing about and starts talking.
  • Ask anything. Interrupt the guide with a question the way you would a person, and it answers.
  • Screen off, phone away. Start a walk, then put the phone in your pocket. Lock-screen controls let you pause, mute the microphone, skip, or ask.
  • Your interests, your language. Choose what you care about and the guide leans into it.
